Compact selection per 100 g. Sources remain independently identified. CoFID
available carbohydrate and measured AOAC fiber are summed as an approximation
of total carbohydrate, where both are available. Trace is represented as zero
during import and missing measurements are kept as null. CoFID alcoholic
beverages (per 100 ml) and rows without all four core values are omitted.
This generated selection is not a new edition of the original datasets.

Brazilian generic foods still use TACO (see TACO-NOTICE.txt). Packaged food
lookup uses Open Food Facts by barcode (ODbL), kept in its own cache and credited
on each record. See https://world.openfoodfacts.org/terms-of-use.
